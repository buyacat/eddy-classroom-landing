What's in the Eddy Classroom platform

Web version for teachers and administrators

Course and lesson prep, working with the library, a test and quiz builder with different question types, viewing results and the session log.

app.eddy.school/library

The whole group takes the test together

The class answers at the same time and the teacher watches a live table: who is on which question, who got it wrong, and what they scored. One button ends the test.

app.eddy.school/session

Easy student access

Once joined to a course, a student can easily browse its materials, complete assignments, and take tests.

app.eddy.school/quiz

Interactive panel and whiteboard

Front-of-class display of presentations, video, documents, 3D models and simulations; draw and annotate directly over the material in real time.

app.eddy.school/board

Analytics and a lesson-effectiveness dashboard

A clear overview of class engagement and material mastery, with recommendations for the teacher based on session results.

app.eddy.school/results

In-lesson game tools

A wheel of names for random student selection, a timer, and a noise meter — simple tools that add dynamics to the lesson.

  • Timer
